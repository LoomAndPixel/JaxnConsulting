Three steps, in this order.

Calling gets a person on it fastest. The email and ticket make sure nothing is lost.

Step 1

Call us immediately

Call (425) 283-7378. Critical incidents are covered under your service agreement.

Step 2

Send a critical email

Email support@JaxnConsulting.com with the subject [CRITICAL] and a short description. It goes straight to our highest-priority queue.

Step 3

Open a Critical ticket

Sign in to the client portal and choose Critical priority, so the SLA timer starts right away.

What counts

What qualifies as critical?

  • A complete server or network outage
  • An active security breach or ransomware event
  • A business-critical system that is fully unavailable
  • Data loss or corruption in progress
  • A complete loss of internet or communications

Target first response for Critical: 1 hour. See the full SLA policy.

Not an emergency?

Open a regular ticket and we’ll respond within your SLA. Every ticket is tracked in the client portal.
