Work / Knowledge Base Lifecycle

A knowledge base people can trust again.

An AI-powered governance system that audits ServiceNow knowledge articles for duplicates, outdated content and misfiled entries, and keeps them current automatically.

Focus

Knowledge accuracy and governance

Platform

ServiceNow Knowledge Management

AI

NLP similarity · ML classification

Reporting

Power BI dashboard

The problem

Thousands of articles, no clear owner.

Over time the knowledge base had grown to thousands of articles, many overlapping, outdated or owned by people who had left. Technicians kept running into:

  • Conflicting or duplicate articles
  • Content filed under the wrong categories
  • Outdated procedures still visible to technicians
  • Slower resolution times and less confidence in the answers

Reviewing it all by hand was impractical. Leadership needed an automated way to keep auditing and refining the knowledge base.

The solution

How it works.

An AI analysis pipeline connects to ServiceNow’s Knowledge Management API, checks every article, cross-references ownership with HR and Active Directory, and routes each one to consolidation, archival or verification.

Key capabilities

What it does.

  • ServiceNow API
  • NLP
  • Machine learning
  • Active Directory
  • Power BI

01

Duplicate detection

NLP similarity scores find near-duplicate and redundant articles.

02

Ownership validation

Article owners are checked against HR and Active Directory to flag retired or inactive employees.

03

Classification accuracy

Machine learning reads keywords and context to catch misfiled articles and suggest the right category.

04

Lifecycle automation

Outdated or duplicate articles are queued for consolidation or archival; validated ones are marked current.

05

Governance dashboard

Power BI shows article health, ownership and classification accuracy for leadership.

Quality without the manual grind.

Continuous analysis keeps the knowledge base accurate without anyone combing through it by hand.
