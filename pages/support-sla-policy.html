Support / SLA policy

Support SLA policy

Our service level agreement for support response and resolution times. These are the commitments behind every ticket.

On this page

  • Response times
  • Business hours
  • How to escalate
  • SLA exclusions
  • Contact

Response times

Business hours

Standard support: Monday–Friday, 9 AM–5 PM CT. Critical incident support is available as set out in your service agreement.

Tickets submitted outside business hours are handled the next business day, unless they’re flagged Critical.

How to escalate

  • Reply to your ticket and ask for escalation
  • Call (425) 283-7378 with your ticket number
  • Email support@JaxnConsulting.com with [ESCALATE] in the subject

SLA exclusions

SLA timers pause during client-caused delays (waiting on credentials, access or approvals), scheduled maintenance windows, force majeure events, and issues caused by third-party vendors outside Jaxn Consulting’s control.

Contact

Email support@JaxnConsulting.com or call (425) 283-7378.
